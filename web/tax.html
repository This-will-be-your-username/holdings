What's modelled

  • Income years run 1 July – 30 June. Distributions fall in the year of their date — use the tax year field for a June-quarter distribution paid in July, which is assessed in the earlier year.
  • Rates are resident marginal rates for the year ({{printf "%.0f" .FirstRate}}% first bracket for {{.FY.FY}}) plus a flat 2% Medicare levy, applied to the investment income stacked on top of your other income.
  • Franking credits offset your tax and any excess is refunded. The foreign income tax offset is capped at the Australian tax on that income once it exceeds $1,000.
  • CGT uses actual parcels (each buy or reinvestment is its own parcel, cost base includes brokerage, foreign trades converted at the transaction-date rate). A 50% discount applies to parcels owned more than 12 months; losses are applied to non-discount gains first, then discount gains, and carry forward automatically. Tax-deferred distributions reduce the cost base, and any excess becomes a gain (event E4).
  • CGT reform from 1 July 2027 (now law): the 50% discount is replaced by CPI indexation of the cost base for growth after that date, with a 30% minimum tax on that growth; gains built up before 1 July 2027 keep the discount. This page calculates sales under the current rules. Use the Sell estimator to compare the same sale before and after the change. Sales recorded on or after 1 July 2027 are still calculated under the current rules here.
  • Not modelled: shares held in a company, trust, SMSF or joint names; wash-sale/trading-stock rules; foreign-currency hedging; non-resident or part-year residency; deductions such as accountant fees.
