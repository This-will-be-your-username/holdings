About this estimate

  • Sale price defaults to the latest price (US holdings are converted at today's AUD rate; override either). A future date uses today's prices.
  • The 50% discount applies to parcels owned more than 12 months. Capital losses (including ones carried forward from earlier years) are applied to full-rate gains first, then to discount gains.
  • Not tax advice. See the Tax page for what's modelled — including the proposed 2027 change to the CGT discount that isn't reflected here.
