Before or after the 1 July 2027 CGT change?

now law

The 50% discount is replaced from 1 July 2027 (Tax Reform No. 1 Act 2026). The gain that built up before that date keeps the discount; growth after it is taxed on a CPI-indexed basis with a 30% minimum rate. This prices the same sale both ways, assuming each holding's cost base resets to today's value on 1 July 2027.

Assumptions and what isn't settled yet
  • Each holding's value just before 1 July 2027 is taken as today's price, so the gain to date is the discounted "deferred" gain and the new cost base starts there. Real values on that day will differ.
  • Growth after 1 July 2027 ({{printf "%.1f" .B.A.GrowthPA}}% a year) and CPI ({{printf "%.1f" .B.A.CPI}}% a year) are your assumptions; indexation can't create or increase a loss. With 0% growth the new rules cost the same as the old ones — the difference appears as soon as prices rise.
  • Indexation and the discount on the earlier gain are applied only to parcels owned more than 12 months at the sale, counted from the original purchase date. How the 12-month test works after the 1 July 2027 reset hasn't been published; if it restarts, indexation would be lost on younger parcels.
  • Capital losses are applied to the pre-change (deferred) gains first, then to new gains — removing today's choice that preserves the discount. The 30% floor applies only to the growth after 1 July 2027. Exemptions (e.g. income-support recipients), trusts and the ATO's guidance on valuing assets are not modelled; further legislation is still being drafted.
  • Sales recorded after 1 July 2027 on the Tax page are still calculated under the current rules.
