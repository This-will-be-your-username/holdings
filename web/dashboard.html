How these numbers are calculated

  • Cost basis uses average cost (brokerage included). Foreign holdings are converted to AUD at the transaction-date rate.
  • Return = unrealised + realised + income (+ franking credits, switchable on the Tax page), divided by the money you put in. "Realised" here is average-cost; the Tax page works out the taxable gain using actual parcels.
  • XIRR is the annualised, money-weighted return of your actual cash flows (buys out, sells and distributions in, current value at the end) — so it reflects your timing, unlike a fund's published return. Shown once there's 30+ days of history.
