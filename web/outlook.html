How this works

  • Past behaviour: for each holding the app resamples its own real daily price moves from up to 5 years of history (the same days for every holding, so they move together as they have before) to build thousands of possible six-month paths. The bands are the 50% and 80% ranges of those paths.
  • Growth assumption: "Balanced" averages the last year's growth, the full-history growth and a 6% long-run assumption (each capped), then adds a small market-trend tilt (±1.5% a year) when the market is clearly above/below its 200-day average. Switch the assumption above to see how much the answer depends on it — it usually matters more than anything else.
  • Current conditions: volatility is scaled up or down by how choppy the last 60 days have been compared with the long run. The market panel shows the ASX 200, S&P 500, volatility (VIX) and the AUD/USD rate, which moves the AUD value of US holdings.
  • Trend line: the dashed line is a straight-line fit of the last year of the holding's (log) price, extended forward — a summary of the recent direction, not a forecast. "Fit" near 1 means a steady trend; near 0 means choppy.
  • Not included: distributions you'll receive, your future buying or selling, tax, news, and events outside history (crashes can exceed the bands). Managed funds need around 120 unit prices before they can be modelled. Prices are in AUD; US holdings include currency moves.
